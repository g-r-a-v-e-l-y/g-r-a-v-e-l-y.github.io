I thought I wanted to do 3D stuff when I grew up and so I taught myself 3D Studio (R2, for dos. Layers, lofts, and tweening.) in ‘94 or ‘95 by, among other things, mocking up a Fly by eyeballing an ad for them in Guitar Player Magazine of Gabrels holding one.

As soon as I was making any money, about ten years later, I bought one [https://pizza.slow.high.quality.gravely.pizza/parker-fly-classic/](https://pizza.slow.high.quality.gravely.pizza/parker-fly-classic/).
